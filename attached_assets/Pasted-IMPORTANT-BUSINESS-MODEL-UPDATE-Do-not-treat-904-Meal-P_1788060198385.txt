IMPORTANT BUSINESS MODEL UPDATE:

Do not treat 904 Meal Prepz as a traditional ecommerce store where customers browse individual products and place unrelated products into a normal shopping cart.

This business operates using a WEEKLY PREORDER MEAL PREP MODEL.

Every week approximately 15 meals are offered across categories such as Breakfast, Entrées/Lunch/Dinner, Healthier Options and Premium Meals.

Customers select quantities of meals from THAT WEEK’S menu.

Example customer order:

#4 × 2
#5 × 2
#6 × 1
#7 × 1
#8 × 2
#10 × 2

Redesign the ordering experience around this workflow.

Create a section called:

BUILD YOUR WEEK

Display the current weekly meals grouped by category.

Each meal should include:

* meal number
* meal name
* photo
* description
* calories
* protein
* carbs
* price
* premium indicator when applicable
* minus quantity button
* current quantity
* plus quantity button

Do NOT use traditional “Add to Cart” buttons.

Customers should simply increase/decrease the quantity beside each meal.

Create a sticky/floating Your Week summary showing:

* total meals selected
* each selected meal
* quantity
* meal subtotal
* premium meal charges
* running subtotal

Then create a multi-step ordering flow:

STEP 1 — BUILD YOUR WEEK
Customer chooses meals and quantities.

STEP 2 — FULFILLMENT
Customer chooses Pickup or Delivery.

Pickup should support configurable weekly pickup windows.

Delivery should support configurable delivery zones/fees and customer address entry.

For the current demo, make delivery pricing editable/configurable rather than permanently hard-coded.

STEP 3 — CUSTOMER INFO
Name
Phone
Email
Address if delivery
Optional order notes

STEP 4 — REVIEW ORDER

Show:

* selected meals
* quantities
* meal subtotal
* premium charges
* delivery charge
* final total

STEP 5 — SECURE CHECKOUT

For this demo, prepare the architecture for Square hosted checkout but do not require live payment credentials.

WEEKLY MENU ARCHITECTURE:

The menu changes EVERY WEEK.

Do not hard-code meal cards individually throughout components.

Create one portable structured weekly-menu data source inside the repository.

Suggested:

/src/data/weeklyMenu.ts

or equivalent JSON.

Every meal should contain structured fields such as:

id
mealNumber
name
category
description
price
calories
protein
carbs
image
premium

The entire menu UI should render dynamically from this one source.

Make replacing the complete weekly menu extremely easy.

All meal images must remain in portable public assets such as:

/public/images/meals/

Never store them in Replit-specific storage.
